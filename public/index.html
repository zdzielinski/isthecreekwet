    Data source: Hourly rainfall from the Dugout Ranch weather station, provided by the Utah Climate Center at Utah State University. Forecast from the National Weather Service (NOAA). Times are Mountain Time.

    How data is retrieved: The station reports once an hour. To keep load on the Utah Climate Center low, we fetch its data at most once an hour and serve that saved copy to every visitor until it expires. Loading this page doesn't contact the Utah Climate Center directly, and the numbers shown can be up to about an hour behind the station. The forecast is saved and refreshed the same way, at most once an hour.

    Visiting Indian Creek: The BLM asks climbers to wait at least 24–48 hours after rain before climbing on sandstone, because wet rock erodes faster and can weaken anchors and holds. Some crags are voluntary avoidance areas for nesting raptors from March through late summer. Pack out human waste, stay on existing trails to protect the cryptobiotic soil, and respect private land. See the BLM's Indian Creek page for current raptor closures, camping, and fees.

    Support climbing access: Donate to the Access Fund, which protects climbing access across the U.S., or the Southern Utah Climbers Alliance, which works with land managers on access, trails, and replacing aging bolts and anchors.

    Hosting: This site runs on Cloudflare Workers. The backend is Python, running in WebAssembly in Cloudflare data centers around the world, so there's no server to maintain. Station and forecast data are stored in Cloudflare's edge cache, which is how the site checks the Utah Climate Center at most once an hour no matter how many people visit.
